# Texas Hold'em - Quick Play Guide

## 🎯 Objective
Win chips by making the best 5-card poker hand using your 2 hole cards and 5 community cards.

## 🎮 Game Flow

### 1. **Pre-Flop** 
- Blinds posted: You pay $10 (small), Dealer pays $20 (big)
- You receive 2 hole cards (private)
- **Your Turn**: FOLD / CALL $20 / RAISE
- **Dealer Turn**: Responds to your action

### 2. **Flop** 
- 3 community cards revealed
- New betting round starts at $0
- **Actions**: FOLD / CHECK / RAISE

### 3. **Turn**
- 4th community card revealed
- Another betting round
- **Actions**: FOLD / CHECK / CALL / RAISE

### 4. **River**
- 5th and final community card revealed
- Final betting round
- **Actions**: FOLD / CHECK / CALL / RAISE

### 5. **Showdown**
- Both hands revealed
- Best 5-card hand wins the pot!
- Click "NEW HAND" to play again

## 🃏 Betting Actions

| Action | When Available | What It Does |
|--------|----------------|--------------|
| **FOLD** | Always | Surrender the hand, lose all bets |
| **CHECK** | When current bet = your bet | Pass without betting |
| **CALL** | When current bet > your bet | Match the current bet |
| **RAISE** | Always (if you have chips) | Increase the bet |

## 🎲 Raise Controls
- Use **-** and **+** buttons to adjust raise amount
- Minimum raise: $20 (big blind)
- Maximum raise: All your chips (all-in)

## 🏆 Hand Rankings (Best to Worst)

1. **Royal Flush** - A♠ K♠ Q♠ J♠ 10♠
2. **Straight Flush** - Five cards in sequence, same suit
3. **Four of a Kind** - Four cards of same rank
4. **Full House** - Three of a kind + pair
5. **Flush** - Five cards of same suit
6. **Straight** - Five cards in sequence
7. **Three of a Kind** - Three cards of same rank
8. **Two Pair** - Two pairs
9. **One Pair** - Two cards of same rank
10. **High Card** - Highest card wins

## 💡 Tips & Strategy

### **Pre-Flop**
- ✅ Raise with: Pairs, A-K, A-Q, high cards
- ⚠️ Call with: Medium pairs, suited connectors
- ❌ Fold: Low unmatched cards (2-7, 3-8, etc.)

### **Post-Flop**
- If you hit a pair or better: **Raise or Call**
- If you have a draw (4 to flush/straight): **Call small bets**
- If you have nothing: **Check or Fold to bets**

### **Reading the Dealer**
- **Dealer checks**: Likely weak hand
- **Dealer raises**: Strong hand or bluff
- **Dealer calls**: Drawing or medium hand
- **Dealer folds**: Very weak hand

### **Pot Odds**
- If pot is $100 and call is $20: Good odds to call with draws
- If pot is $40 and call is $60: Need a strong hand to call

## 📊 Game Info Display

- **Your Chips**: Top left in green
- **Pot**: Center in red
- **Dealer Chips**: Top right in red
- **Phase**: Shows PREFLOP / FLOP / TURN / RIVER
- **Current Bet**: How much you need to match
- **Your Best Hand**: Shows below your cards (when 3+ community cards)
- **Recent Hands**: Bottom section shows last 5 hand results

## 🎊 Special Features

### **Auto-Reload**
- If either player runs out of chips, both get $1000 automatically
- Keep playing without interruption!

### **Hand History**
- Tracks your last 5 hands
- Shows result and pot size
- Learn from your wins and losses

### **Best Hand Display**
- Automatically calculates your best possible hand
- Updates as community cards are revealed
- Helps you make informed decisions

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

**Can't check?**
- You can only check when current bet equals your bet
- Otherwise, you must CALL or FOLD

**Raise button disabled?**
- You don't have enough chips for the raise amount
- Use - button to lower raise amount

**Game seems stuck?**
- Wait for dealer's turn (800ms-1000ms delay for realism)
- Click "NEW HAND" after showdown to continue

## 🎯 Winning Strategy

1. **Play tight pre-flop** - Fold weak hands
2. **Be aggressive with strong hands** - Raise to build the pot
3. **Watch dealer patterns** - Adjust to their playing style
4. **Manage your bankroll** - Don't go all-in every hand
5. **Learn hand values** - Know what beats what
6. **Position matters** - You act first = more info needed
7. **Bluff occasionally** - Keep dealer guessing
8. **Chase draws cautiously** - Consider pot odds

## 🎮 Quick Command Reference

```
STARTING HAND:
- Strong: Raise (AA, KK, QQ, AK, AQ)

- Weak: Fold (7-2, 8-3, etc.)

AFTER FLOP:

- Draw: Call small bets
- Miss: Check or Fold

- Checks → Weak
- Raises → Strong or Bluff

- Folds → Very Weak
```
